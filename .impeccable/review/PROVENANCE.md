# Review evidence provenance

All PNG files in this directory are real Electron application screenshots generated by `desktop/tests/capture.mjs` or `desktop/tests/live-readonly.mjs`. `desktop-*` and `compact-*` use explicitly labelled test-transport data; they do not represent hardware validation. `live-readonly-*` use the actual Rust sidecar with `--stdio --safe-mode`, actual USB enumeration and native audio inventory, without audio/HID mutation requests.

Device photographs embedded inside these development screenshots are © SteelSeries, downloaded by the actual opt-in application path from the fixed URL in `desktop/src/shared/artwork-catalog.ts`. They show Arctis Nova 7 Gen 2, black variant. Transparent margins were trimmed without cutting the headset or receiver. Manufacturer art is not covered by the project's code license and is not bundled in the production renderer. These screenshots document the UI; they confer no artwork redistribution rights.

The six visual review captures were inspected in two bounded passes. First-pass issues (undersized photo due to transparent margins, small operational copy, discontinuous dark rail in full-page captures and cramped Unmanaged selector) were corrected together. Final vision confirmation found prominent unclipped photos, no overlaps and complete rails. Detector ran once in degraded regex mode because parser packages were absent; its one warning was spring-ish fader easing, intentionally pinned by the brief. The independent finish reviewer belongs to the parent integration workflow.
